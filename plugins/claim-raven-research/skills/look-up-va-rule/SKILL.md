# Look up a VA rule

1. Call `lookup_va_rules` with the citation or the rule the person describes.
2. Quote only the text it returns, with the citation and the date Claim Raven last confirmed it. If it comes back stored or unavailable, say so with its date.
3. Explain the rule in plain words after the quote.

This is general information from public VA law, not a reading of anyone's file and not legal advice. A free Claim Raven account adds your own claim, your VA.gov copy and condition research: https://claimraven.com/mcp
