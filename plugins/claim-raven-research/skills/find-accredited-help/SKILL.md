# Find accredited help nearby

1. Ask for the state and, if they will share it, a city or ZIP code, and whether they want free help from a veterans service officer, an attorney or a claims agent.
2. Call `find_accredited_help` with the `state`, `city` or `zip`, and the `kind` of help.
3. List what it returns as it comes back. Do not rank or recommend anyone. Those marked as in your area are in the city or ZIP code given; the rest are from elsewhere in the state, and the list is not sorted by distance.
4. Say that help from a veterans service officer is free. Give the fee rule as the notice states it: in general, an accredited attorney or claims agent may charge only for work after VA's notice of its initial decision, under a fee agreement, and older appeals follow different rules.
5. Give the link to VA's accreditation search so the person can confirm anyone before signing a form naming them.

Claim Raven does not recommend or endorse anyone listed. This is general information, not legal advice.
