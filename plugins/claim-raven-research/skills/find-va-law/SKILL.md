# Find the VA law on a question

1. Call `find_va_authority` with the question in plain words.
2. Call `lookup_va_rules` for the full text of each rule it names that matters to the answer.
3. Answer in plain words, citing each source with its citation and date, and quoting official text only from these results. Say plainly what you could not check.

This is general information from public VA law, not a reading of anyone's file and not legal advice. A free Claim Raven account adds your own claim, your VA.gov copy and condition research: https://claimraven.com/mcp
