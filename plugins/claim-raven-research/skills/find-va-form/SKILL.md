# Find the right VA form

1. Call `find_va_form` with the `form` number if the person named one, or a plain description of the `need`.
2. For each form, say what it is for and when VA wants it, and mention when another form fits better.
3. If it returns the decision review options, they matter only when VA has already decided the claim. Ask if you do not know. If it has, explain the three ways to ask for another look, and the one-year timing, before suggesting one form.
4. Give the official va.gov link and the link to fill it out free on Claim Raven.

Claim Raven never submits a form. The person reviews, signs and sends it. This is general information, not legal advice.
