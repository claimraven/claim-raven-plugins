# Claim Raven Pro Research

Research-only VA law and Board of Veterans' Appeals plugin for accredited representatives and attorneys with a [Claim Raven Pro](https://pro.claimraven.com) account. This connection reaches no client or member records; it provides read-only access to Board of Veterans' Appeals decisions and outcome figures, condition research, 38 CFR, 38 U.S.C., M21-1 and M21-5 text, and VA authority lookups.

## Included skills

- `board-case-research`: pull Board grant, denial and remand figures, study winning decisions, and cite relevant opinions with dates.
- `condition-theory-research`: map direct, secondary, presumptive and aggravation pathways, rating criteria, and exam evidence.
- `va-authority-brief`: assemble statutes, regulations and manual provisions with verified text and last-confirmed dates.

## Setup

Install in Claude Code:

```
/plugin install claim-raven-pro-research --marketplace claimraven/claim-raven-plugins
```

Then sign in with your Claim Raven Pro account through Claim Raven Connect (`https://mcp.claimraven.com/mcp`). Do not paste client names, file numbers or client records into your AI app unless your agreement with the AI company covers them.

Claim Raven is independent from VA. Research output is general information and not legal or medical advice.
